MLPro Continues at Its New Home
===============================================

Dear MLPro Community,

we would like to let you know that MLPro is moving to a new organizational home. The current version **2.1.0** marks the final MLPro release published under the stewardship of **South Westphalia University of Applied Sciences, Germany**.

From now on, the project will be continued by `blueAIC UG (haftungsbeschränkt) <https://www.blueaic.com>`_ in the dedicated GitHub fork:

- `blueaic/mlpro <https://github.com/blueaic/mlpro>`_

With blueAIC taking stewardship of the project, we aim to ensure the **long-term, sustainable maintenance and further development of MLPro at a professional level**.

Apart from this change in stewardship, **nothing changes for the MLPro community**. MLPro will continue as a free and open-source project under the Apache 2.0 license and will remain available as usual through:

- `Online Documentation <https://mlpro.readthedocs.io/>`_
- `PyPI <https://pypi.org/project/mlpro/>`_

A new release is already in preparation and will be published soon.

Please note that **RSS feeds will no longer be supported** for future MLPro updates. Instead, you can subscribe to the MLPro Newsletter:

- `MLPro Newsletter <https://www.blueaic.com/newsletter-mlpro>`_

This repository and RSS feed will now be frozen and retained as part of MLPro's project history.

We look forward to continuing our collaboration with the MLPro community and to many more contributions, ideas, and developments in the years ahead.
